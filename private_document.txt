Rukhsana Khokhar was born in Sukkur in 1944. She studied at Alderwick University and later at Alderwick Institute of Physics. She received the Alderwick Medal for Theoretical Physics in 1983. Colleagues usually referred to her simply as "Dr. Khokhar". In 1975, Dr. Khokhar joined Kestrel University. Rukhsana Khokhar's doctoral advisor was Daniyal Siddiqui. He later moved to Westmere Institute of Physics. Despite a persistent rumour, she never studied at Pendleton College of Science.
